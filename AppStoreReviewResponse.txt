Guideline 1.4.1 - Safety - Physical Harm

DailyDil does not connect to external medical hardware and does not provide medical services through a hardware accessory.

The previous binary included an optional Bluetooth device path. That path has been fully removed:

- There is no Bluetooth pairing, scanning, or GATT client in the app.
- NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription and NSBluetoothPeripheralUsageDescription have been removed from Info.plist. The app does not request Bluetooth permission.
- Measure and Home no longer offer device measurement, pairing, battery, firmware, or “simulate device” controls.
- Capture is limited to phone camera wellness screening: fingertip PPG with the rear camera and torch, and facial rPPG with the front camera.

Because the app no longer uses designated medical hardware:

- We are not attaching regulatory-clearance documents for hardware, because no hardware is used.
- We are not attaching a hardware test report or peer-reviewed hardware study, because no hardware is used.
- We are not restricting storefronts for hardware clearance, because no hardware is used.

DailyDil is a wellness and screening app. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. Readings are wellness snapshots from the iPhone or iPad camera, not clinical measurements.

Guideline 2.1 - Demo video of hardware pairing

A pairing demo video is not applicable to this binary.

There is no designated hardware, no pairing flow, and no accessory interaction to film. The current version of the app runs only on the Apple device using the built-in camera.

If App Review still wants a walkthrough of the current product, we can provide a video of onboarding, a fingertip camera screening, a facial rPPG screening, and viewing a saved wellness reading on a physical iPhone or iPad. Please let us know if that would help.

Notes for testing this binary

- No demo account is required. The app has no login.
- On first launch, complete onboarding (camera is explained; Apple Health is optional and can be skipped).
- Use Measure → Finger PPG or Facial rPPG. These are camera wellness screenings only.
- Bluetooth is not used. The app should not prompt for Bluetooth.
